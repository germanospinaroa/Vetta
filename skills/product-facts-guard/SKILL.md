# Product Facts Guard

Read `COPY_APPROVED.md` and `PRODUCT_CONTEXT.md` before reviewing a page. Check product name, modules, lessons, benefits, bonuses, price, guarantees, certifications, testimonials, economic promises and included content.

For every claim, record the source and status (`approved`, `pending`, `conflict`). If a value is absent, mark it pending. Never infer or complete missing facts. A price must match exactly `US$12.98` and `Pago único` for SAVORA. Never approve a fictional checkout URL. Never approve `Purchase` instrumentation without a real purchase confirmation source.
