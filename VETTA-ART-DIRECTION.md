# VETTA — Art Direction Explorations

## Purpose

This document records the visual research and the two deliberately different directions exposed at `/concepto-a` and `/concepto-b`. They are explorations only. The approved project `DESIGN.md`, funnel, copy, APIs and data contracts remain unchanged until a direction is selected.

## Verified references

1. **Neutra VDL Studio & Residences** — https://neutra-vdl.org/
   - A numbered information architecture, editorial scale and architectural photography make the site feel like a place with a point of view.
   - Derived for VETTA: a visible sequence marker, quiet navigation, and one continuous spatial composition instead of repeated cards.

2. **Viens-là** — https://viens-la.com/
   - The live site treats brand, narrative, services, guided exploration and playful interaction as one authored system.
   - Derived for VETTA: each concept needs one recognizable interaction language and copy should remain part of the art direction.

3. **Mouthwash Studio** — https://mouthwash.studio/
   - Its live information architecture connects narrative, creative direction, visual identity, motion and digital design rather than presenting isolated features.
   - Derived for VETTA: the visual device must explain the experience, not decorate it.

4. **Siteinspire — architecture category** — https://www.siteinspire.com/websites/category/architecture
   - The gallery exposes a broad range of typographic, grid, unusual-layout and photography patterns from architecture practices.
   - Derived for VETTA: use a measured grid as a brand instrument; let alignment and proportion carry authority.

5. **Awwwards — recent nominees and award references** — https://www.awwwards.com/websites/single-page-1/
   - The collection shows how typographic systems, scrolling, transitions, photography and unusual navigation can coexist when each has a role.
   - Derived for VETTA: keep motion short and legible; use one signature transition instead of a catalog of effects.

6. **Awwwards live presentation reference: Viens-Là / Editorial New** — https://assets.awwwards.com/assets/files/live-presentation.pdf
   - The case material explicitly discusses layout, typography, content architecture, scroll-revealed content and mobile legibility.

7. **Land-book studio references** — https://land-book.com/voovstudio
   - The platform documents brand, packaging and web work as a connected commercial identity practice.
   - Derived for VETTA: avoid a generic “marketing page” look; the wordmark, labels, tone and CTA should belong to the same system.

## Direction A — Cinematic Editorial

**Thesis:** VETTA feels like a considered film about the moment before a decision: a human figure is never shown as proof, but light, depth and framing create the sense of attention being directed.

- Palette: midnight blue, mineral ivory, oxidized copper and a restrained signal red.
- Type: Manrope for navigation and action; Instrument Serif for the emotional line only.
- Signature move: a vertical “decision reel” that changes from many blurred frames to one sharply framed frame.
- Composition: asymmetric hero with a large authored visual field, a narrow editorial rail and a visible sequence.
- Motion: slow entrance of the frame, copper line draw, small opacity/transform reveals. No autoplay video or scrolljacking.
- Mobile: image field becomes a framed portrait ratio, copy remains above the CTA, the sequence line becomes horizontal.

## Direction B — Contemporary Architecture

**Thesis:** VETTA is a decision instrument: alternatives occupy a visible field, and the experience progressively establishes one clear axis without pretending the decision is mathematical.

- Palette: warm chalk, structural black, electric ultramarine and a controlled orange-red.
- Type: Manrope for the system; a condensed or mono treatment for coordinates and labels; serif is used only as a small counterpoint.
- Signature move: the “choice plane,” an original SVG construction whose axes align with the information hierarchy.
- Composition: modular grid, diagonal datum, oversized coordinate labels and a floating registration module.
- Motion: planes translate by a few pixels, grid lines draw in, selected states invert. All movement is transform/opacity based.
- Mobile: the grid becomes a vertical spine; planes stack as readable sections, never as an inaccessible canvas.

## Shared non-negotiables

- Same offer, promise, CTA, Tuesday/Thursday structure and registration fields.
- No invented dates, hosts, results, urgency, testimonials, scarcity or prices.
- No public internal implementation language.
- No external image or font dependency added for the explorations.
- No tracking events are fired by the concept routes.
- Both routes use `noindex` and are excluded from the sitemap.
- Reduced motion renders the same content in its final readable state.
